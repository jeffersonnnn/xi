Vote for the 2026 FIFA World Cup Starting XI with your $XI tokens.

$XI is the People's Starting XI — a live, community-driven lineup for the biggest sporting event in history. Connect your Solana wallet, pick one player per position in a 4-3-3 formation, and watch the community's consensus squad update in real time.

HOW IT WORKS:
- Hold $XI tokens in a Solana wallet
- Connect your wallet on $XI
- Pick your Starting XI — one player per position
- Sign a message to submit your vote (no gas fees!)
- Your vote is weighted by your $XI token balance
- The People's XI updates live as votes come in

THE 10-MINUTE REFRESH:
Vote weight isn't static. Every 10 minutes, the app re-checks every voter's on-chain token balance. The Starting XI shifts in real time with player substitution animations.

FEATURES:
- Live football pitch visualization
- Player cards with nationality, club, and vote weight
- Real-time substitution animations
- Full player roster with search and filtering
- Voter leaderboard
- Track how your picks are performing
- World Cup kickoff countdown

BUILT ON SOLANA:
Every vote is cryptographically signed and verified. No transactions, no gas fees. Your vote is tamper-proof and publicly verifiable.

The 2026 World Cup starts June 11, 2026. Your squad starts now.
