## Description (4000 chars max)

Vote for the 2026 FIFA World Cup Starting XI with your $XI tokens.

$XI is the People's Starting XI — a live, community-driven lineup for the biggest sporting event in history. Connect your Solana wallet, pick one player per position in a 4-3-3 formation, and watch the community's consensus squad update in real time.

HOW IT WORKS:
• Hold $XI tokens in a Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or any Solana wallet)
• Connect your wallet on $XI
• Pick your Starting XI — one player per position
• Sign a message to submit your vote (no gas fees!)
• Your vote is weighted by your $XI token balance
• The People's XI updates live as votes come in

THE 10-MINUTE REFRESH:
Vote weight isn't static. Every 10 minutes, the app re-checks every voter's on-chain $XI balance. Buy more tokens, gain more influence. Sell your tokens, lose your weight. The Starting XI shifts in real time with player substitution animations — just like a real match.

FEATURES:
• Live football pitch visualization with the current People's XI
• Player cards with nationality, club, and vote weight
• Real-time substitution animations when position leaders change
• Full player roster with search and position filtering
• Leaderboard showing most influential voters
• "My XI" view to track how your picks are performing
• Countdown to the 2026 World Cup kickoff

BUILT ON SOLANA:
Every vote is cryptographically signed and verified using Ed25519. No transactions, no gas fees — just a signature. Your vote is tamper-proof and publicly verifiable.

The 2026 World Cup starts June 11, 2026. Your squad starts now.
