## What problem do you solve?

Fan engagement in global football is passive. Supporters debate squad selection endlessly on social media, but there's no structured, high-stakes mechanism to aggregate and display collective fan opinion. Prediction markets come close but focus on match outcomes, not squad composition.

$XI creates a real-time, stake-weighted governance system where token holders vote for the Starting XI. The result is a living, community-driven lineup that shifts as opinion and token ownership change — a product that's impossible to build without blockchain.

## Why is your solution better than alternatives?

- **Not a prediction market**: We're not betting on outcomes — we're aggregating squad preferences with weighted governance
- **Real-time**: The XI updates every 10 minutes as token balances shift, creating a dynamic product that incentivizes ongoing engagement
- **Zero friction**: No gas fees, no staking, no lock-ups — just a wallet signature
- **Visual**: A live football pitch with player cards and substitution animations makes the output shareable and intuitive
- **Crypto-native distribution**: Token holders have natural incentive to promote and engage

## Market Opportunity

The 2026 World Cup will be the largest sporting event in history (48 teams, 3 host nations, projected 5B+ cumulative viewers). $XI is positioned as the definitive fan-governance product for this moment. No competitor combines token-weighted voting with real-time squad visualization.
