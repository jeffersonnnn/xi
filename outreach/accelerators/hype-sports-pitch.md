## One-Line Pitch

Solana token holders vote for the 2026 World Cup Starting XI. Your bag picks the squad.

## The Problem

Billions of football fans have opinions about who should start in the World Cup. These opinions are scattered across social media, podcasts, and bar conversations with no structured way to aggregate, weight, or display collective fan preference in real time.

## The Solution

$XI is a token-weighted governance platform where holders pick the 2026 FIFA World Cup Starting XI. Each voter's influence is proportional to their on-chain $XI token balance. The result is a live, community-driven lineup displayed on an interactive football pitch with real-time updates and substitution animations.

Key differentiators:
- Vote weight = token balance (refreshed every 10 min)
- No gas fees — just a wallet signature
- Live pitch visualization with player cards
- Mobile app for iOS and Android

## Why 2026 World Cup?

- 48 teams (expanded from 32)
- Hosted in US, Canada, Mexico
- Projected 5B+ cumulative global viewers
- Most commercially significant World Cup ever
- US market exposure at unprecedented scale
