# $XI — One-Pager

## The Problem

Fan engagement in the World Cup is passive. Billions of people watch, but their opinions — who should start, who should be dropped — live and die on social media with no stakes, no aggregation, and no permanence.

## The Solution

$XI is a token-weighted voting platform where holders pick the 2026 FIFA World Cup Starting XI. Each voter connects a Solana wallet, signs a message selecting 11 players (4-3-3), and the community's consensus lineup updates in real time. Vote weight = on-chain token balance. The Starting XI shifts as tokens change hands.

## Why Now

- **2026 World Cup**: Largest sporting event in history — 48 teams, hosted across US/Canada/Mexico, ~5 billion cumulative viewers
- **Crypto-native fan engagement**: No existing product combines token governance with mainstream sports
- **Solana consumer moment**: The ecosystem is actively funding and promoting consumer-facing dApps

## How It Works

1. Hold $XI tokens in a Solana wallet
2. Connect wallet on worldcupxi.xyz
3. Sign a message picking one player per position
4. Your vote is weighted by your token balance
5. The "People's XI" updates live, with substitution animations when leaders change
6. Balances are re-checked every 10 minutes — sell your tokens, lose your influence
