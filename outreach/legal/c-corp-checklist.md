# Delaware C-Corp Formation Checklist

## Why Delaware C-Corp?

- Standard structure for crypto/tech companies
- Required by most accelerators and VCs
- Clear legal framework for token projects
- Separates personal liability from project
- Required for trademark filing under a business name

## Step-by-Step (Stripe Atlas)

### 1. Prepare

- [ ] Choose a company name: e.g., "XI Labs Inc." or "Peoples XI Inc." or "$XI Inc."
- [ ] Decide on your role: Founder, CEO, Sole Director
- [ ] Have your personal info ready: legal name, address, SSN (or ITIN for non-US)
- [ ] Decide on equity split (if co-founders): standard is equal split with 4-year vesting

### 2. Apply

- [ ] Go to https://atlas.stripe.com
- [ ] Fill in company details
- [ ] Pay $500
- [ ] Sign documents electronically

### 3. What You Get (within 48 hours)

- [ ] Delaware C-Corp Certificate of Incorporation
- [ ] EIN (Employer Identification Number) from the IRS
- [ ] Bylaws, stock purchase agreements, IP assignment agreement
- [ ] Mercury bank account (auto-opened)
- [ ] 83(b) election template (important for founder stock — file within 30 days!)

### 4. Post-Formation

- [ ] **File 83(b) election within 30 days** (critical for tax purposes if you're issuing founder stock)
- [ ] Update trademark filing to use company name
- [ ] Open a crypto-friendly bank account if Mercury doesn't meet needs
- [ ] Set up bookkeeping (Bench, Pilot, or a CPA)
- [ ] File Delaware Franchise Tax annually (minimum $400)
- [ ] File Federal/State tax returns

### 5. The Tweet

> This isn't a memecoin. It's a company building the future of fan engagement.
> EIN obtained. Bank account open. Let's build.
