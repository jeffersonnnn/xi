## What is $XI?

$XI is a token-weighted voting platform on Solana where holders select the 2026 FIFA World Cup Starting XI. Connect wallet → sign message → pick 11 players in a 4-3-3 formation. The community's consensus XI updates live, weighted by each voter's on-chain $XI balance (refreshed every 10 min).

No gas fees. No staking. Just sign and vote.

---

## Why Solana?

- Ed25519 signatures for gasless vote verification
- SPL token balance reading for live vote weighting
- Fast, cheap token transfers enable dynamic governance (weight shifts as tokens move)
- Solana's consumer-app ecosystem aligns with mainstream sports engagement
