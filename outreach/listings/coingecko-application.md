**Project Description (Long)**:
$XI is a community governance platform where Solana token holders vote for the 2026 FIFA World Cup Starting XI. Each holder connects their wallet, signs a message selecting one player per position in a 4-3-3 formation, and the aggregated "People's XI" is displayed on a live football pitch visualization.

Vote weight equals your current on-chain $XI token balance, refreshed every 10 minutes. If you buy more tokens, your influence grows. If you sell, your weight drops. The Starting XI updates in real time with player substitution animations whenever a position's leader changes.

No gas fees to vote — just a signature. No staking, no lock-ups. Your bag picks the squad.

Built on Solana with Ed25519 signature verification, Helius RPC for on-chain balance reading, and Privy for wallet authentication.
