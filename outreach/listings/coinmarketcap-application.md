## Project Information

**Project Name**: $XI

**Token Symbol**: XI

**One-liner**: Token-weighted voting for the 2026 FIFA World Cup Starting XI on Solana

**Detailed Description**:
$XI is a community governance platform where Solana token holders vote for the 2026 FIFA World Cup Starting XI. Holders connect their wallet, sign a message selecting one player per position in a 4-3-3 formation, and the community's consensus lineup updates in real time on a live football pitch visualization. Vote weight equals on-chain $XI balance, refreshed every 10 minutes. No gas fees, no staking — just sign and vote.
